Do patterns travel? Printed pattern books and the Icelandic manuscripts

Pink lines join a printed book to a manuscript when both hold the same figure (19 figures so far, matched cell by cell allowing turns, mirroring and a cell of difference; see books/travel). Grey dashes: copying the museum records between books. Pink = charts we hold, teal = open scans not yet digitised, white = metadata only (51 books: we know the date, place and holder but have no usable scan, or must ask for access). Circles on the map grow with the number of books printed in that place. Book list with sources and each library's terms: books/survey.csv and books/library_terms.csv. Bars run from first edition to the edition held; Icelandic bars are the date range on record (undated manuscripts are left off the timeline but kept on the map).
