# Multi-symbol chart from issue #9

The scan reader described in [PR #47](https://github.com/HiDefTextiles/Sjonabok/pull/47) and implemented in [PR #49](https://github.com/HiDefTextiles/Sjonabok/pull/49) works from page images, so it does not require an EPS export. `MultiSymbolPlateSource` reads the first image attached to [issue #9](https://github.com/HiDefTextiles/Sjonabok/issues/9) as six ruled chart bands, each 115 columns wide on one shared column grid.

The second image attached to the issue (`issue9-example-2.png`) is not another layout: it is what the old EPS pipeline made of this same page, a 0/1 picture in which the mixed symbols came out as solid blocks and stray dots. It is kept as the "before" for comparison; the band heights it shows (11, 6, 8 and 7 rows for bands 2 to 5) match the reader.

The source screenshot and its extracted chart grids are included here. The visible marks are X, diamond, circled cross, and half triangle. The `.symbols.txt` layers use `0` for empty cells and share codes `1`–`4` across all six bands, ordered by each mark's total frequency in the page. Rotated and mirrored instances are grouped as the same shape. The neighbouring `.txt` files retain the reader's binary filled/empty layer.

Page-wide symbol totals are `1: 3,153`, `2: 2,045`, `3: 938`, and `4: 618`. `issue9-example-1-preview.png` stacks the six colour-rendered symbol grids, preserving each scanned glyph's orientation while tinting it by its page-wide ID. Smaller individual previews are available for [band 1](issue9-example-1_r1-preview.png), [band 2](issue9-example-1_r2-preview.png), [band 3](issue9-example-1_r3-preview.png), [band 4](issue9-example-1_r4-preview.png), [band 5](issue9-example-1_r5-preview.png), and [band 6](issue9-example-1_r6-preview.png), which are easier to open on mobile. Each banner heading reports its strongest repeated horizontal offset (at least three cells), agreement among marked positions, and the fraction of positions marked on either side. With the shared lattice, every band agrees on 98 to 100% of marked positions; tests require at least 97%. Regenerate the grids and previews from the scan with `python patterns/multicolor/render_issue9.py`. Hand-drawn pages may need a separate grid or line-drawing strategy.
